    FAQ

    Good to know

    Can you shoot and edit it for us?

    Yes. We're a social media agency too: we can script, shoot, edit and sound your content, as a one-off project or a monthly package.

    Can I visit the studio before booking?

    Of course. Send us a message and we'll set up a free 15-minute walkthrough.

    What's included in the hourly rate?

    The space, basic lighting, Wi-Fi, the makeup area and access to the lounge. Extras such as pro lighting or catering can be added to a quote.

    How do I pay?

    A 30% deposit secures your slot and the rest is paid on the day. We accept card, transfer and cash.

    What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

    You can reschedule for free up to 48 hours before your slot. If you cancel later than that, the deposit is kept.

    Is there parking?

    Yes, there's free street parking and a paid lot two minutes away.
